# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 16, 2026**

Welcome to iQ, developed and operated by Serene Interactive, Global (SiG). This policy describes how iQ handles your data. We may update this policy periodically; changes will be posted here with an updated date.

## About Serene Interactive, Global

Serene Interactive, Global is an AI research and development company. We build software and AI systems with privacy, accessibility, and sustainability at their core.

## Exception-Based Audit Model

iQ processes all interactions in real-time and discards them immediately after response generation. We do **not** store conversations, prompts, or responses as standard practice.

However, to maintain system stability and resolve critical failures, iQ collects **anonymized error traces** when critical events occur.

### What We Log

We only log **Critical Events**, which include:

- **System Failures:** API timeouts, crashes, logic loops, or technical errors
- **Safety Triggers:** Any time iQ refuses a prompt due to safety filters (only safety metadata is logged; the original prompt text is not stored)
- **User Reports:** Any interaction where a user explicitly mentions a "bug" or "error"
- **Performance Telemetry:** Anonymized latency and token count data associated with critical events

### What We Do NOT Log

We do **not** log:

- Standard conversational data
- Prompts or queries (unless they trigger a Critical Event)
- Responses generated by iQ
- Personal identifiers or account information

### Why We Log

Critical Event logs are maintained solely for:

- Diagnosing and resolving system failures
- Monitoring safety trigger patterns
- Maintaining overall service stability
- Compliance with regulatory requirements

All logged data is anonymized by default and used solely to maintain system stability and resolve critical failures.

## Data Storage and Retention

### Standard Data

All standard conversational data is:
- Processed in real-time only
- Discarded immediately after response generation
- **Not retained in any form**

### Critical Events

Critical Event logs are:
- Retained for a maximum of **7 days**
- Used solely for diagnostics, debugging, and system stability
- Securely accessible only to authorized SiG personnel
- Automatically deleted after the retention period

### Rationale for 7-Day Retention

The 7-day retention period balances:
- **Technical troubleshooting:** Sufficient window for diagnosing and resolving critical issues
- **Privacy protection:** Minimizes exposure of operational data
- **Security:** Reduces attack surface if logs are compromised

## Children's Privacy

## Data Sharing

- Serene Interactive, Global (SiG) does **not** share your data with any third parties.
- **Standard conversational data:** No data is stored, so no data is shared.
- **Critical Event logs:** May be shared with:
    - SiG leadership for audit purposes
    - Security teams for incident response
    - Technical teams for system improvement
    - Legal counsel when required by law or for compliance

## Security

- While standard conversational data is not stored, security measures are in place to protect the integrity of iQ and its infrastructure, prevent unauthorized access, and comply with regulatory requirements.
- All diagnostic logs and critical event data are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Access controls restrict log access to authorized development personnel, and logging systems are monitored for security events.
